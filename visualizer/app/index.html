Gas Network Explorer

Open an SDDP gas-network case

Pick the case folder (the one that contains celecnode*.dat, celectransport.dat and the result CSVs), or drag folders onto this window. Files are read locally in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

To compare cases, open several folders. To share a pre-loaded case with your team, see README.md.
